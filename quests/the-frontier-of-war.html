The Frontier of War

After gaining advantage over the Zivcas forces, Covenant faction must also start taking care of the Inflow area and face the Frontier troops. You are ordered by the Raguel and Metropolite to eliminate 3 squads of Frontier faction. Frontier squads can be found in the Inflow area next to the outposts occupied by the Frontier. Each squad has around 5 to 6 soldiers.
