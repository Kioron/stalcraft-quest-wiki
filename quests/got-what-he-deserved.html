Got What He Deserved

After speaking with Outpost Commander you head to the given location on the map. Among the trees you will find unknown stalker, you must ask him if he has seen Coxcomb passing by. The unknown stalker will give you another direction in which Coxcomb ran off. You must follow the marker to catch the Coxcomb. After reaching the location, you find Coxcomb dead. You must examine his body, you will see that his throat was cut, which means that he was killed by someone else, not mutants. You must go back to the BoatHouse and tell Saint about Coxcomb's death.
