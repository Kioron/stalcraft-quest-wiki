Figth with the Master of the Zone

After eliminating the Zivcas troops at Forge-11 base, you head to the underground laboratory of the Zivcas. There you are met by Raguel, in the Master of the Zone's armor. You must defeat him. Due to the highly advanced armor he fully protected and only has some weak spots. First you must destroy the four generators to take of his protective shield. You need to use a lot of flashbang grenades to stun him and be able to deal damage. Raguel will have weak spots on his chest, back and arms. You must avoid his railgun and cluster grenades.
