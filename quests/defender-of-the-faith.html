Defender of the Faith

Raguel tells you to support and help the trusted individuls: Chistopher, Tretyak and Vasily Maslinkin. You must find them inside the Covenant base and speak to each of them. They will give you the tasks that must be completed for them.


Christopher will tell to inspire some of the Covenant members to join the fight and take next missions. To convince one of the member, you must bring back his lost icon. For the other covenant member, you must give him the correct speech following rules from the ammendments.


You must convince Afon to speak with Vasily. To do that, you must find the Kir's body, who was Afon's friend, and then Afon will speak with Vasily. Kir's body is located in the north of the Covenant base, next to the Sawmill.


Tretyak is located at the top level of the Covenant base, you must use the staircase to reach him. Tretyak tells you to find out merchants who are withholding their offerings, which negatively impacts Covenant faction.
