Old Buddy

Saint tells you that last night, Art fled into the swamps and Kalgan went after him, now they are both missing. Kalgan is a friend of Saint and he worries about it, he tells you to find Kalgan and if anything happened to him, Art is the one to Blame.

You receive the marked area on your map, you must head to the location and enter the underground hideout.



You find that Kalgan is dead and Art is standing next to him. After Art spots you, he will become hostile and attack you. You must finish him off. After the fight, pick up the PDA from Kalgan's body and return it to Saint.
