False Face

You are ordered by Raguel to get to Orpheus and steal his laptop with data. You will be given the Zivcas gear and be disguised as one of the Zivcas soldiers during the fight, which will allow you to easily reach Orpheus without raising the alarm. After reaching the Orpheus, you will need to knock him out and steal his laptop. There will be a broken wall in the room, which you can jump out through. After jumping out, Frontier members will also join the fight against Zivcas and Covenant. You must head back to the camp location eliminating Frontier troops on the way.
