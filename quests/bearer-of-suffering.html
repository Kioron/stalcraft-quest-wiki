Bearer of Suffering

After weakening the ranks of the Frontier, Metropolite decides to create alliance with the mercenaries faction. He wants to futher expand into the center of the map - Forge-11 base occupied by Zivcas troops. You must join the Afon to attack one of the Zivcas outposts in the center of the map and reach the Zivcas' laptop to install a virus. After clearing the base, you must get access to the laptop in the office, break into it and install the virus, which will share Covenant propaganda among all communication sources of Zivcas.
