Orderly Day

After completing both tasks, Jaundice sends you the location of the Orderly base and tells you to come and see with him. Before you go, empty your inventory and take only necessarry items as you may lose them during the fight in this location. While getting close to the location, watch out for the mines, as they are placed around next to the entrance.



After speaking with Jaundince, you will have to choose to kill or save Linden, who has been captured by orderlies. If you choose to save Linde, all Orderlies inside the base will attack you and you will must fight them. After clearing the top level of the base, remember to take the Orderly documents from the table in the Jaundice's office. After that go back the way you came inside the base, clear the rest of the Orderlies and leave.
