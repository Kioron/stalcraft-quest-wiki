Brother

After finding out that Oracle is missing, Condor sends you his location and tells you to bring him back. You must travel to the Oracle's location, which is located in the north direction of the Condor's base in the abondoned village. He sits in one of the houses on the bed in one of the rooms. After reaching him, he will tell you he escaped because he did not want to bring anymore danger to Condor's people. After persuading him to return back to Condor, he will give you more information about the Blue Sphere.
