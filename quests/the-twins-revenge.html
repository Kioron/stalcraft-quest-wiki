The Twins Revenge

After reaching Afon, you enter the facility full of Zivcas forces. You must eliminate all Zivcas soldiers on the way and reach the office at the end of the building. Inside the office there's going to be a teleport, which you must enter.


After entering the teleport, you will be teleported to the swamp, which you must navigate through before reaching the Twins location. The navigation is going to be hard, as the visibility is very low due to the fog and there's no map turned on. You must follow the ghosts pointing the correct direction to get out of swamp.


When reaching the final teleport, you will be teleported to the Twins location and you will have to fight them. The boss fight is seperated into 2 phases. The first Twin moves on the catwalk with a sniper rifle and can't come down to you, he also has limited visibility and range and he may only shoot you when you are in the open area, which is mainly in the center of the facility. Good tactic is to fight the first Twin, who has access everywhere and follows you with a shotgun, as then you do not have to fight with two of them at once. After spawning in their base, go to the left corridor and get on a box, as Twin will struggle to successfully shoot you. It is a great spot to easily kill the First Twin.


After killing the first Twin, the second Twin will be a much easier target. You must take a peak on him and just start shooting. He will not deal enough damage to quickly kill you, so you will have time to hide, reload and heal easily. After killing the Twins, you will speak with Metropolite who will wake up from unconsciousness and enter the teleport in the middle of the main room to get back to the base.
