Capturing the Outpost

After wiping out some of the Frontier's forces, the next task to weaken their ranks is capture one of the outposts on the map. Outpost event starts randomly, the base needs to be wiped out of the forces that occupy it and will be successfully captured if enough points are scored. To score the points, players must be in the range of outpost, once the faction reaches 100% of score, the outpost becomes the faction's property.
