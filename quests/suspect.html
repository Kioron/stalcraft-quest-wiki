Suspect

After speaking with Probe, Viper and Coxcomb you collect information about them and you are able to suggest a motive for each of them. You must report back to Saint and speak with him.



During the conversation with Saint you will be given 3 options to choose from: accusing Probe of being a murderer, accusing Viper or pointing out Coxcomb. The third option is going to be the correct choice and will progress to the next mission.
