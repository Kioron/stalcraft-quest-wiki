Looking for Receiver

After getting to the location of the signal, you will need to find what is causing it. You will need to climb the ladder to get to the roof of the house. After climbing the ladder, you will need to crawl through the small gap to reach the attic. Inside the attic, you will find Kaa who will give you 4 riddles to solve before giving you the location of the Receiver artifact.
