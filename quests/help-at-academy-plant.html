Help at Academy Plant

You must attack the Zivcas base with Afon south of the Forge-11 location. The base is highly fortified, to destroy the bunkers you will need to use the grenade launcher, as normal bullets are innefective. After advancing to the center of the base you will need to clear even more forces and fortifications. After reaching the end of the base you will find proffesor Sollyanikov, who was captured by Zivcas from his lab.
