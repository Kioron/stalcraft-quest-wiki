Roman and David

Raguel tells you to head to the campsite of Roman and David and investigate it, after finding that Metropolite was with them during their last moments. After arriving at the place of encampent and investigating the campfire, you will see the past events that occured between Roman, David and Metropolite. You must follow their path to understand what happened during that event.
