Voices in the Swamp

After collecting 4 plants to help Metropolite, you are given the task of finding the last 5th plant and return back to the base. While looking for the last plant, you start to hear the voices in your head. You must follow them, you will be given marks on the map on where to head. After reaching the end location, the voices stop, you must find out why they brought you here. Climb up to the top of the building and find the hidden backpack. Inside of it you will find a mysterious note, after that you must head back to Raguel.
