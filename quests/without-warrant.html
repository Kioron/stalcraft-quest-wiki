Without Warrant

After speaking with Saint, you are told to search the Coxcomb's house. After entering the house, head to the room to the left and search a cupboard. Examine it, then examine the magazine and you will find the keys to a crate. Come back to the first room and open the crate. Inside you will find the torn-off ear that belongs to Scuba. After finding the ear you will receive the next quest.
