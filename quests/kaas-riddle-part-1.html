Kaas Riddle Part: 1

Kaa doesn't give you the exact location of how this riddle needs to be solved, so you will not a given any marker on the map. The riddle description says you must reach the top of something high to see the Pit location better and this is the place where Kaa left a hint. That suggests you must reach a tower in the middle of the Pit as that is the highest peak in this location. After reaching the tower you will need to get inside of it and climb the ladder to reach the roof of the building the tower stands on. Then you will need to get to the center of the tower and climb to the third floor of the tower using the pillar. After reaching the correct floor you will see a large backpack, after interacting with it you will find a note with the next Riddle.
