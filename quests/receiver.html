Receiver

After receiving the signal detector from Professor, you will be given the location which you must search using the device. To use the device you must press "X", which will bring up the wide range detector UI. You need to select the middle range and start searching for the signal. After couple of seconds, the signal will be found, you will also see the percentage of the signal, which tells you how close you are to the correct location. If you will walk the correct direction the signal will increase. After getting to the location of the signal, you will need to find what is causing it.
