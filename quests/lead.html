Lead

After receiving more information about the Blue Sphere from Oracle, you must travel to the Pit. On the marked location, there will be an underground banker, which you must enter. Inside of it you will find an abondoned lab. After reaching the end of the corridor and entering the room, you will meet professor Solyannikov. After speaking with the professor, you will tell him that you are one of the Blessed Ones and that you are looking for the Blue Sphere. Professor can help you if you will bring him the artifact called a receiver. The artifact will enable Professor to jam the signal coming from the Zone and locate the Blue Sphere. Receiver can be found using wide range detector, which is given to you by the Professor. After receiving the detector, you must head of from the bunker and find the signal in the Pit.
