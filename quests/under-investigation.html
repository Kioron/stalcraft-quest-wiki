Under Investigation

Saint gives you another task of finding out who killed Scuba. He gives you three suspects: Probe, Viper and Coxcomb. All of them are in the village area. Viper will tell you that he saw Coxcomb and Scuba hanging around together. Probe will give you no useful information, he is not really interested and involved in the case. Coxcomb however, will act suspicious. When asked about Scuba, he will lie and say that he was not with him, which will start the next quest.
