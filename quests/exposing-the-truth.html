Exposing the Truth

You must head to the Cordon location, which is the second zone location on the whole game map. You must reach Photon-2 bandit base and find Cobbler. The Cobbler is sitting at the end of the base in the corner. While trying to find out if he is the Blessed one, you must trick him that you want to help the Blessed Ones, which is going to be one of the dialogue options. After choosing the correct dialogue option, Cobbler will want to get vodka from you. You must give him 3 bottles and then ask him if he is the Blessed one. If you will give him more than 3 bottles, he will become too drunk and you will must wait before being able to speak with him again. After that, he will reveal that he is the Blessed One.
