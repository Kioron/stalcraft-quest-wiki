The Underbelly

After teleporting to the location, first turn left and go to the bridge. Watch out for other players as it is a PVP zone and other players might also spawn in. After reaching the bridge climb up on to it and go to its end. Drop down to the pit under the floating tower and enter the teleporter bubble.


After entering the teleport, you will spawn in some random tower. You will need to leave the tower by climbing down the cliffs. While dropping down, follow the glowsticks, as they will be placed on each platform. At the last cliff, you will jump on to the roof of the floating house. Each place you should will have a glowstick on it, indicating the correct direction. After landing on the roof, head to the right and follow the flares. You will have the pit in front of you, with dogs that will try to attack you. Pass the pit and reach the ladder, without killing the dogs, as they have unlimited amount of respawns, so there is no point of fighting them.


After passing the pit, you will reach the ladder to a construction crane. Climb up the ladder, run through the top of the crane and jump down to another cliff platform. Follow the flares, remember to not stop and another group of psydogs will spawn and chase you. You will need to keep on running and climbing up higher to reach another ladder.


After reaching the top of the tower, you will need to kill the beast, which is the dog located at the end of the tower. It is sitting inside the cage, it can't attack you however, it sends a lot of psydogs to protect itself. You must to kill the dog inside the cage and you will be teleported out of the beast lair. After killing the beast, head back to the Oracle and speak with him.
