Beast

After delivering PDAs to Condor, he will let you speak with the Oracle. Oracle will tell you some information about the Blue Sphere, but he will also reveal, that Condor's squad is tracked down by a beast that communicated with the Oracle. Oracle is also the blessed one and has visions very often. During that time the beast communicates with him and he can't stop that from happening. Because of that, the beast knows his location and sends mutants to fight the Condor's group all the time. You are asked by Oracle to find the beast's lair and kill it. The location of beast's lair is in Agroprom location.


Remember to not bring anything that you do not want to lose, as once you will die in this area, all items that are not bounded to you will get lost and you will not be able to retrieve them. To enter the location of the beast, you must crawl into the dog house. It will teleport you to the location of the beast's lair.
