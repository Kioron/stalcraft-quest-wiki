End of the Hunt

After killing the beast you are teleported out of his lair. Now you must head back to Oracle and tell him that the beast is dead. After coming back to the Condor's base, you find out that Oracle is missing. Condor will tell you that he has got a tracker on Oracle and sends you the location, where to find him. Condor tells you to bring him back.
