Plants and Nightmares

After collecting medical plants and obtaining mysterious note you must head back to the Covenant base and speak with Raguel. You explain to him what happened and what you have found, after showing the note, Raguel tells you it is Metropolite's handwriting. Before investigating this case more, you are ordered to support Afon during the attack on the Zivcas base who are occupying your territory.
