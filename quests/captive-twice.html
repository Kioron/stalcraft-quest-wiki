Captive Twice

After speaking with Jaundice assistant, you must reach the Hole Punch, who is hiding in the Village in the middle of the map, next to the railway. Watch out for other players as it is a highly active PVP zone. Also a lot of NPC camps do spawn in there, which may involve additional fights and risk. After reaching Hole Punch, you may choose to kill him or tell him to cut his ear off, which will make Orderlies believe he is dead. After that that, go back to Jaundice assistant and deliver him the ear.
