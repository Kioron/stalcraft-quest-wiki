Salt in the Wound

You must return back to the lab and speak with the professor. You can use the teleport at the bottom of the Kaa's hideout to get closer to the Pit. Then you must reach the lab and find the Professor. After entering the lab, you find out the Professor is gone and he has likely been kidnapped. After we find out what happened, Shaman calls us and tells us to meet with him.
