Double Agent

Linden gave you information about Orderlies faction and told you to join their ranks and become a spy. You are given a location of Jaundice - faction recruiter. You must go to the location and speak with him to join their faction. After that you will be given two tasks to prove you are worth joining the Orderlies faction.
