The Word of Lord

After clearing the Zivcas base out of its troops, you get access to the Zivcas laptop, with all communication channels. You must install the Virus with the Covenant's propaganda, but first you must break into the laptop. In the first part, you must change line to from key == true to key == false. In the second part you must change line 5 and set it to "true, true, true". In the third part, you must change line 9 to Class.Data.Count and then line 13 to user permission == false.
